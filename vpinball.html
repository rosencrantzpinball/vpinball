  • Table folder: the folder with your table (a .vpx, a .vpz, or an unzipped .vpz pack) and its extras, like pinmame, music or PUP videos.
  • Table file: a .vpx or .vpz table on its own, without extras, or a .vpxz bundle, which is a zipped table folder with its extras.
  • Saving: in Chrome and Edge, changes like high scores, NVRAM and table settings are saved back to the table folder you opened. Other browsers, and table files, keep these changes only until the page is closed. App settings are kept in every browser.
  • Touch: hold the left or right half of the screen to flip. Hold Launch to pull the plunger back, and let go to shoot. Coin, then Start, are along the top, with Menu for the settings. No sound? The silent switch mutes it.
  • Whole screen: add the page to your Home Screen (in Safari's Share menu), and open it from there.
